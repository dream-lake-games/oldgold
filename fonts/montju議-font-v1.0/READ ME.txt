Montjuïc (v.1.0)

ABOUT:

Montjuïc is a pixel-art  font letter that I did for practice type design.  
I didn't use reference during the proces, but I've been clearly influenced 
by fonts I like. For instance; "Gill Sans" or "Montserrat".
